Western & Southern Financial Group is a Fortune 500 diversified family of financial services companies headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Founded in 1888, the group offers life insurance, annuities, mutual funds, asset management, retirement planning, and investment management through a family of subsidiaries including Western & Southern Life, Columbus Life, Gerber Life, Integrity Life, Lafayette Life, Fort Washington Investment Advisors, Touchstone Investments, and W&S Financial Group Distributors, with over $112 billion in assets under management.
